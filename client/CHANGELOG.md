## 0.1.0

First release: a tray app that keeps the bundles you pick in sync, signs in through the
browser with a token of its own, and speaks up only when a person is needed — a conflict
kept aside, an ingest that failed, a token that stopped working. The command-line client
is included, for machines without a desktop.
